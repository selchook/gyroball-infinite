Our gyroball-style game collects minimal data to provide gameplay functionality:

- **Game Progress**: Current level, high scores, and game settings stored locally in your browser
- **Performance Data**: Frame rates and error logs for game optimization (anonymized)

**Note**: No personal information is collected by the game itself. All data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.
